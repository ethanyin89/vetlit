# FIP：瑞德西韦加 GS-441524，缓解 81%
*Thirty-two cats with effusive or non-effusive feline infectious peritonitis treated with a combination of remdesivir and GS-441524*
Green J 等. *Journal of veterinary internal medicine* 2023. DOI [10.1111/jvim.16804](https://doi.org/10.1111/jvim.16804) · PMID 37403259
`猫` `FIP` `CEBM 4 级` `已读摘要`

## 结论
32 只 FIP 猫（含眼部和神经型）用瑞德西韦加口服 GS-441524，12 周末 81% 临床和生化缓解。
