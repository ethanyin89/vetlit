# FIP：莫努匹韦 42 天，约四分之三持续缓解
*Outcomes of a 42-day course of molnupiravir in cats with feline infectious peritonitis: a prospective descriptive study*
Hishinuma K 等. *Journal of veterinary internal medicine* 2026. DOI [10.1093/jvimsj/aalag268](https://doi.org/10.1093/jvimsj/aalag268) · PMID 42949438

## 结论
19 只 FIP 猫口服莫努匹韦 42 天，14 只（74%）持续缓解 ≥6 个月；3 只复发后重新治疗有效。
