# CKD 犬：尿氨/肌酐比低预示死亡和进展更快
*Association of impaired ammonia excretion with survival in dogs with stable chronic kidney disease*
Harris AN 等. *Journal of veterinary internal medicine* 2026. DOI [10.1093/jvimsj/aalag100](https://doi.org/10.1093/jvimsj/aalag100) · PMID 42207576
`犬` `CKD` `CEBM 3 级` `已读摘要`

## 结论
稳定期 CKD 犬中，尿氨/肌酐比 <2.0 者死亡风险约为 3 倍，中位生存 189 天 vs 445 天。
