## 1. 一句话结论 + 证据定位
**一句话结论**：ACVIM 2020 猫心肌病指南：有 CHF 史且左房中重度扩大的猫推荐氯吡格雷 18.75 mg/猫 q24h 预防血栓（LOE low）。

**Oxford CEBM 2011 等级**：不适用（指南）　**偏倚/质量评估工具**：AGREE II　**报告规范**：RIGHT（指南报告）
标签：`指南 · ACVIM · Delphi`

**指南自身推荐强度/证据等级（原文照录，未重新分级）**：
- "Prophylactic antithrombotic treatment with clopidogrel (18.75 mg/cat PO q24h, with food) is recommended in any cat with a history of CHF and moderate to severe LA enlargement (LOE low)"
- 推荐强度措辞：is recommended / should be considered / may be considered / is not recommended
- LOE：low / medium / high；全文标签计数（我方统计）：low 52、medium 17、high 16
